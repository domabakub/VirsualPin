# Photograph sources

These are downloaded visual assets for real phin instruments and the selected
Sketchfab model. No generated images, background replacement, synthesized details,
or AI upscaling are used. Assets are served locally; Next.js may resize/compress
them for delivery.

- Institution: The Metropolitan Museum of Art
- Source: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/500840
- API record: https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/500840
- Original: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/mi/original/midp89.4.288.jpg

The original photographs are modest resolution. They are kept near their native
display size; replace with higher-resolution, appropriately licensed photography
if larger product photography is needed.
